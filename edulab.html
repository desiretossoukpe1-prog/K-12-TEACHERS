Who we are

Education solutions grounded in context.

EDULAB (Education Laboratory) is an education consulting and services initiative focused on helping people and institutions navigate educational challenges and opportunities. We believe effective support begins with listening, understanding local realities, and working collaboratively. Our work is guided by research and by a commitment to improving education across Africa.

Meet the founder

An educator answering a calling.

Fulbright ScholarUniversity Fellow, 2024–2025MA, Teaching English as a Second LanguagePhD student, Educational PsychologyUniversity of ArizonaMultiple scholarship awardeeNearly a decade in the classroom

Désiré Tossoukpe is an educator, researcher and Fulbright scholar. After almost a decade teaching in middle and high school, he came to the United States on a Fulbright Scholarship and earned his MA in Teaching English as a Second Language (TESL). He is now a third-year PhD student in Educational Psychology, with a minor in Higher Education, at the University of Arizona, where he was named a University Fellow for 2024–2025 and has been awarded numerous scholarships in recognition of his academic achievement.

His research explores how program evaluation informs educational policy and how early academic specialization shapes identity and career choices. He also examines what affects access and success for first-generation, low-income (FGLI) students, including sense of belonging and identity development, and he draws on data from across the African continent to shape better solutions for Africa.

He has traveled to conferences to learn from diverse voices in education and has presented his work on education and evaluation. Through AGSA, he supports the African community in building resilience, fostering connection, and paying it forward across the African diaspora worldwide. Beyond academics, he enjoys songwriting, traveling, and geopolitics.

A calling, not just a career

I have seen what a committed teacher can do with very little, and what evidence can do when it is rooted in local reality. EDULAB is my answer to a calling: to use research, experience and community to help improve education across Africa, one learner, one teacher and one school at a time.
